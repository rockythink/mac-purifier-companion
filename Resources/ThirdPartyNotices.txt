Remote Xiaomi product images and catalog metadata
-------------------------------------------------
Resources/model-catalog.json contains display metadata and HTTPS links to
product images on Xiaomi-operated FDS hosts. The images are fetched remotely
at runtime and are not copied into the application or source distribution.
Product names, trademarks, and remote images remain the property of their
respective owners; the project GPL license does not license those images or
trademarks. Public signed image URLs originate in the linked catalog, not in
user credentials. Each identified model records its public metadata page.
The independently maintained Xiaomi Miot Spec index is https://home.miot-spec.com/.
No affiliation, endorsement, or additional image-use permission is claimed.
